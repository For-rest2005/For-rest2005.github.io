I am an undergraduate student majoring in Information and Computational Science at [School of Computer Science](https://cs.nju.edu.cn/mainm.htm), Nanjing University.

I once participated in some research under the guidance of Professor Zhang Yuan and Professor Song Yuhang at Nanjing University, focusing on post-quantum cryptography and brain-inspired computing, respectively. Although both experiences were rather preliminary attempts.

My personal interests in engineering and science are quite broad, generally revolving around these four areas:

* AI infrastructure, which is part of my current work and takes up the bulk of my time
* Finance and economics. 
* Physics, mainly centered on quantum mechanics
* Cognitive science

The first two are fields where I have had relevant work experience, while the latter two correspond to the two research projects I participated in at school.
